An EO-driven fire-risk model for the Eastern Himalayas.

Built a forest-fire risk model fusing SAR backscatter, terrain, and meteorological variables to support mitigation planning in Sikkim. The framework went on to feed ecosystem-resilience indicators adopted in national programme reporting.

Problem

Mountain forest fires don't behave like everyone else's.

Sikkim sits at the seam of subtropical and alpine ecosystems, with steep terrain, monsoonal moisture, and fuel that varies sharply with elevation. Off-the-shelf fire-weather models built for flatter, drier landscapes underperform here — and so does any model that ignores topography or relies only on optical imagery (clouds win most days).

The state needed a risk surface granular enough to support mitigation planning and explainable enough to be defended in a programme review.

Approach

SAR for vegetation moisture, DEM for terrain, meteo for the weather window.

Feature stack

  • Sentinel-1 SAR backscatter (VV / VH) — vegetation moisture proxy
  • SRTM-derived slope, aspect, elevation, TWI
  • Reanalysis temperature, rainfall, RH, wind
  • Distance-to-road / settlement (anthropogenic ignition proxy)
  • MODIS / VIIRS historical fire occurrence (label)

Modelling

  • Pixel-level classification (fire / no-fire)
  • Random Forest baseline, XGBoost final model
  • Class-imbalance handled with stratified sampling + class weights
  • Spatial cross-validation — random splits leak in this geometry
  • Feature-importance reporting for interpretability
Evaluation

Honest metrics on the right test set.

  • F1 ≈ 0.82 on held-out test pixels with spatial separation from training.
  • Confusion patterns reviewed by terrain zone — model was strongest on lower-elevation broadleaf belt, weaker on transitional alpine.
  • Risk surface aggregated to administrative units for use in mitigation planning.
Outcome

From a thesis to a deliverable.

  • Streamlined geospatial workflows for WRI India's SLR programme via standardised ETL and modelling templates.
  • Produced ecosystem-resilience indicators adopted in national programme reporting.
  • Established a SAR-first risk-modelling pattern reused on adjacent CORAL projects (mangrove biomass, shifting-cultivation monitoring).
  • MS (Research) thesis at IIT Kharagpur, CGPA 9.0.
