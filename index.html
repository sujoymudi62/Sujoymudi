About

Where remote sensing meets machine learning.

Built for the messy parts: validation, drift, reproducibility, and getting EO + field data to actually agree.

I'm a Machine Learning & Geospatial specialist with deep expertise in CNNs, U-Net, Transformers, semantic segmentation, and time-series modelling, applied to environmental monitoring, forest carbon, and natural hazards.

My day-to-day spans cloud-native geospatial engineering (STAC/COG, xarray/dask, Earth Engine, AWS/GCP), model validation and drift evaluation, and integrating EO data with field measurements for MRV and carbon assessments.

I care about reproducible pipelines, honest uncertainty, and AI that makes ecological monitoring more trustworthy — the climate-tech and dMRV space is where I want to keep building.
